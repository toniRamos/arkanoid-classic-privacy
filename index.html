Privacy Policy – Arkanoid Classic

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Arkanoid Classic (on iPhone and iPad: “Brick Breaker: Retro Ball”) is a brick breaker arcade game with 400 levels, an endless mode, a nightmare mode and a daily challenge.

Arkanoid Classic does not collect, store, or share any personal data directly. The game does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or location.

However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information automatically to provide their services: Google AdMob and Google Analytics, described below, and on Android also Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy and data protection terms, which protect it at least as much as this policy does, and we do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Advertising

Arkanoid Classic uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display interstitial ads and rewarded ads. These services may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage data
  • Approximate location, derived from the IP address

This information is managed directly by the advertising provider and is subject to their respective privacy policies. You can review Google's advertising practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
How long Google keeps this data is explained here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

If you see an ad that is inappropriate or not suitable for your age, report it with the AdChoices or feedback icon shown on the ad, or write to the contact address below saying what you saw.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any personalised advertising identifier is used. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform. To withdraw or change it, delete the app and install it again: the consent message is shown again on the first launch.

On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow it when iOS asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised and the game works exactly the same, including rewarded ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time from your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Analytics

Arkanoid Classic uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as game events and session information, that help us improve the game. This data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include personal information such as name, email address, or precise location. The events say when a level, an endless run or a daily challenge starts and ends (mode, level, wave, score, time played and lives left), your daily challenge streak and the stars it gave you, which achievements you unlock, whether you answered the rating prompt, and which ads and rewards you saw or used; on Android, also Play Games sign-in, leaderboards and the optional purchase. Two counters travel with them: the furthest level you have reached in the classic mode and in the nightmare mode.

Google Analytics deletes these events automatically when the data retention period of the game's analytics property ends. Deleting the app stops them for good: nothing is sent once it is uninstalled. If you want the anonymous analytics linked to your installation deleted, write to the contact address below and we will ask Google Analytics to remove it.

In-App Purchases

On Android, the optional purchase that removes ads is processed securely through Google Play: payment information is handled directly by Google and is not accessed or stored by Arkanoid Classic. On iPhone and iPad, any purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store, and payment information is handled directly by Apple.

Platform Services

On iPhone and iPad, app distribution is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

On Android, the app may use Google Play Services and Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and achievements), which may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. For more information, please review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Local Data

Level progress and stars, endless mode and nightmare records, daily challenge streak, achievements, unlocked cosmetics and settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion and graphics quality) are stored locally on your device, together with a diagnostic log of the current session – operating system, graphics chip, screen size and language – that is kept on the device and used to investigate crashes. Everything listed here is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

Arkanoid Classic is not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
